    Citizen Help & FAQs

    What is the MPLAD Citizen Reporting Portal?

    This portal allows citizens to independently verify and report anomalies in local development works funded by the Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS). Reports assist supervisory vigilance officers in prioritizing audits.

    Is my report anonymous?

    Yes. Reporting is completely anonymous by default. We do not ask for or record your name, phone number, or national ID. Your GPS coordinates are strictly used to verify the photo was taken at the physical project location.

    Where do I find my Reference ID?

    Upon submitting a report, an 11-character code (starting with CR-) is displayed on your confirmation screen. You can use this ID on the Track Report tab anytime to follow up.

    What happens after I submit a report?

    Your report updates the project's risk score and enters the district auditor queue. A vigilance officer reviews the evidence, conducts on-site checks if needed, and issues corrective notices to implementing agencies.
